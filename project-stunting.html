Project 03 · Analysis

Child Stunting and Socioeconomic Factors

A group academic project on how poverty, education, and other country-level factors relate to child stunting risk.

Overview

The project looks at stunting risk across countries and asks which socioeconomic indicators move together with that risk. The table is small: 307 rows from 77 countries, so the result is an analytical case, not a production model.

What I worked on

  • Business understanding and the project question.
  • Feature engineering for the socioeconomic variables.
  • A Random Forest classifier and a feature-importance review.
  • A Streamlit page used to explain the result.

What the analysis is for

The useful output is the ranking of socioeconomic factors, such as poverty, fertility, literacy, and GDP, not a single accuracy number. Prevalence of stunting was kept out of the predictors so the model would not be explaining the label with a near-copy of the label.
