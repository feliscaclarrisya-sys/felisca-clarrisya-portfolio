Lifestyle Disease Risk

A group academic project that classifies low and high disease risk from lifestyle and basic health indicators.

Overview

The model estimates whether a person falls into a higher disease-risk group from features such as BMI, blood pressure, sleep, daily steps, smoking, and alcohol use. The dataset is synthetic and public, so the app is a methods demo, not a medical tool.

Data

Health & Lifestyle Dataset by chik0di on Kaggle. About 100,000 synthetic rows, CC0 license. This project used 80,000 rows. The target, disease_risk, is imbalanced: roughly three quarters of the rows are low risk.

What I worked on

  • Exploratory analysis of the lifestyle and health features.
  • Feature engineering for blood-pressure ratio, pulse pressure, obesity, low sleep, and a simple risk score.
  • An 80/20 split, with scaling fitted on the training set only.
  • SMOTE applied only to the training set.
  • Logistic regression tuned with grid search, then interpreted through its coefficients.
  • A Streamlit app so the result can be tried without opening the notebook.
