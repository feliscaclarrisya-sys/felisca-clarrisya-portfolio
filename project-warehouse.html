Project 01 · Power BI

Publication Data Warehouse

A group academic project that organized faculty publication data into a star schema and a Power BI dashboard.

Overview

The question was not only how many papers a faculty produced, but whether that output was concentrated in higher-quartile journals. The warehouse was built so publication count, quartile, and collaboration could be compared across faculties and study programs.

What I worked on

  • Cleaning and standardizing publication and lecturer data in Power Query.
  • A star schema with fact and dimension tables for multidimensional analysis.
  • DAX measures for publication count and Q1–Q4 share.
  • Dashboard pages for trends, quartiles, and cross-program collaboration.

Result

A rising publication count did not always mean a higher share of Q1 journals. The dashboard made that gap visible by faculty and study program.

Tools
