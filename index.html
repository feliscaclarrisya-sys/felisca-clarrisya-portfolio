Publication Data Warehouse

Group project on faculty publication data from 2020 to 2025. The question was why some faculties publish more papers without raising their share of Q1 journals.

Measures used in the dashboard: PublicationCount, Publications_Q1 to Q4, and Q1_Share.

What I worked on

  • Cleaning lecturer and publication fields in Power Query.
  • Star schema with fact and dimension tables.
  • DAX measures for publication count and Q1 share.
  • Dashboard pages for trend, quartile mix, and collaboration.

Finding

A higher publication count did not automatically mean a higher Q1 share. The dashboard separates volume from journal quality.

Socioeconomic factors in the discussion

  • Poverty rate
  • Fertility rate

Poverty and fertility were the strongest socioeconomic signals in the write-up. Stunting prevalence was not used here as a predictor, because it is too close to the label.
